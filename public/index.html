    THE PRODUCT IDEA

    AI for the note.
    People for the care.

    Gemini is used where multimodal language models are helpful — turning an image and a person's own story into structured, understandable context. A fixed safety layer keeps diagnosis and treatment decisions out of scope.

    01 / NOTICE
    ◉

    Describe, don't diagnose

    Gemini vision returns structured ABCDE descriptors — asymmetry, border, color, relative diameter, evolution — plus photo-quality limits. Never a condition name, never a cancer label.

    HUMAN-IN-THE-LOOP BY DESIGN
    SAFETY BY DESIGN

    What SpotStory
    will not do.

    This hackathon build is a communication prototype, not a screening or triage product.

    ×

    No diagnosis or condition names.
    It never labels a spot as melanoma, basal cell carcinoma, or any other condition — and never gives an “all clear.”

    ×

    No treatment or medication advice.
    It never recommends a treatment, drug, or home remedy.

    ×

    No authority over urgency.
    Its “prompt / routine” triage hint only frames the conversation; a clinician decides how soon care is needed.

    ×

    No claim of clinical validation.
    Real-world use would require expert review, fairness testing, privacy work, and appropriate regulatory assessment.

    ◈

    Live mode data flow: the browser re-encodes the photo on a local canvas (stripping EXIF/GPS metadata and checking focus), then sends that resized image and your selected context to this local server; the server calls the Gemini API with a secret environment variable and returns structured JSON. Nothing is written to disk — no images, no notes. Google processes the API request under its applicable terms — review them before using any real personal health information.
