THE PRODUCT IDEA

AI for the note.
People for the care.

Gemini is used where multimodal language models are helpful — turning an image and a person's own story into structured, understandable context. A fixed safety layer keeps diagnosis and treatment decisions out of scope.

SAFETY BY DESIGN

What SpotStory
will not do.

This hackathon build is a communication prototype, not a screening or triage product.

×

No diagnosis or “all clear.”
It never estimates whether a spot is cancer.

×

No urgency decision.
It does not decide how soon care is needed.

×

No saved health record.
The demo app does not persist your photo or notes.

×

No claim of clinical validation.
Real-world use would require expert review, fairness testing, privacy work, and appropriate regulatory assessment.

◈

Live mode data flow: the browser sends a resized image and your selected context to this local server; the server calls the Gemini API using a secret environment variable and returns structured text. The server does not write images to disk. Google processes the API request under its applicable terms — review them before using any real personal health information.
